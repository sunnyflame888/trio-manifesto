The Name
名字

FORMA. Latin for form. Architecture's first principle. The word contains everything: the shape of a garment, the structure of a day, the solution to a life that refuses categories. Ye would say it is the SOLUTION made word. Anderson would say it is the object that needs no explanation.

FORMA。拉丁语的"形式"。建筑的第一原则。这个词语包含了一切：衣服的形状，一天的结构，对拒绝分类的生活的 solution。Ye 会说这是被制成词语的 SOLUTION。Anderson 会说这是不需要解释的物品。

We chose a four-letter word for the same reason Ye chose YEEZY: brevity is power. But we chose Latin for the same reason Anderson curates exhibitions: history is the only authentic foundation. FORMA is not invented. It is excavated.

我们选择四个字母的词语，和 Ye 选择 YEEZY 的理由相同：简短就是力量。但我们选择拉丁语，和 Anderson 策展展览的理由相同：历史是唯一 authentic 的基础。FORMA 不是被发明的。它是被挖掘的。

FORMA is not a brand. It is a question asked in the form of clothing: What remains when everything unnecessary has been stripped away?

FORMA 不是一个品牌。它是一个以衣服形式提出的问题：当一切不必要的东西被剥离后，还剩下什么？

01

Five Pillars
五根支柱

01 / CURATION

The Archive

Every piece is selected, not designed. Like Anderson at Loewe, we edit rather than invent. Each garment must earn its place in the wardrobe.

02 / SOLUTION

The Form

Like Ye's SOLUTION-based approach, every piece solves a problem: the meeting that becomes a match, the day that refuses compartments.

03 / ARCHITECTURE

The Space

Clothes as architecture for the body. Silhouettes derived from space, not tradition. The concrete walls of Silvestrin meet the ceramic vessels of Rie.

04 / CRAFT

The Hand

Craft as oxygen, not decoration. The maker's name on every label. The slight irregularity that proves a human was here.

05 / VOID

The Ruin

Beauty in what remains. Dystopian minimalism meets curatorial precision. The color of concrete, the weight of survival.

06 / SOVEREIGNTY

The Creator

The wearer as creator. No logos that own you. No trends that define you. You build your own uniform, one piece at a time.

The modern man does not live in compartments. His morning begins with coffee and code, his afternoon with a backhand down the line, his evening with a negroni and conversation. Three scenes. One day. One body. Why should he change his skin three times?

现代男人不生活在隔间里。他的早晨从咖啡和代码开始，下午从一个反手直线球开始，晚上从一杯 negroni 和 conversation 开始。三个场景。一天。一个身体。为什么他要换三次 skin？

— FORMA Atelier
02

The Object
那件物品

Every brand needs its Puzzle bag. Its 350. The object that contains the entire argument. FORMA's foundational piece is the Forma Jacket: half charcoal wool, half technical membrane. The seam runs diagonal from shoulder to hem, hand-stitched with visible thread. Not reversible. Not convertible. Coexistent.

每个品牌都需要它的 Puzzle 包。它的 350。那件包含全部论点的物品。FORMA 的 foundational piece 是Forma Jacket：一半是炭灰羊毛，一半是技术膜面料。接缝从肩膀到下摆对角线延伸，手工缝制，线迹可见。不是双面穿。不是可转换。是共存。

Wear the wool side for the boardroom. Wear the membrane side for the court. Or wear it as it is — the seam exposed, the contradiction celebrated. Ye would call this the SOLUTION to the wardrobe. Anderson would call it the only object you need to understand the entire brand.

羊毛面朝前穿去董事会。膜面料面朝前穿去球场。或者就按它本来的样子穿——接缝外露，矛盾被庆祝。Ye 会称之为对 wardrobe 的 SOLUTION。Anderson 会称之为理解整个品牌唯一需要的物品。

This jacket is not a solution to a problem. It is a question that becomes the solution: Why did we ever believe that a man must choose between being dressed and being free?

这件夹克不是一个问题的 solution。它是一个变成 solution 的 question：我们为什么曾经相信，一个男人必须在"穿着得体"和"自由"之间做选择？

The Color of Ruins / 废墟的颜色

Ye's apartment had no colors except concrete, stone, and light. Anderson spent hours with Chinese monochrome glazes. FORMA's palette is the intersection: archaeological colors excavated from what remains.

Ye 的公寓除了混凝土、石头和光线，没有别的颜色。Anderson 在中国单色釉前度过了数小时。FORMA 的色板是两者的交集：从 remaining 中挖掘的考古颜色。

Void 虚空
Charcoal 炭灰
Concrete 混凝土
Ochre 赭石
Terracotta 赤陶

These are not fashion colors. They are the colors of survival. What persists when everything else has been stripped away. The color of concrete at dusk, of sand after rain, of bone bleached by sun.

这些不是时尚颜色。它们是生存的颜色。当其他一切都被剥离后，persist 的东西。黄昏时混凝土的颜色，雨后沙子的颜色，被太阳漂白的骨头的颜色。

The Manifesto
宣言

FORMA is not clothing for multiple occasions. It is clothing for one occasion: the occasion of being alive, moving through spaces, refusing to be categorized.

FORMA 不是为多种场合设计的衣服。它是为一种场合设计的衣服：活着的场合，穿过空间的场合，拒绝被分类的场合。

We do not believe in fashion seasons. We believe in chapters. Each chapter is a conversation with a different craftsperson, a different material, a different question. Season One: Portuguese ceramicists for the buttons. Season Two: Japanese indigo dyers. Season Three: Scottish knitters. The maker's name appears on the label. This is not marketing. This is Creator Sovereignty.

我们不相信时尚季节。我们相信 chapters。每一章都是与不同工艺师、不同材料、不同问题的对话。第一季：葡萄牙陶艺师制作纽扣。第二季：日本靛蓝染工。第三季：苏格兰编织工。制作者的名字出现在标签上。这不是营销。这是创造者主权。

We do not believe in logos that own you. We believe in forms that serve you. The FORMA mark is subtle, architectural, optional. Like Ye's approach to branding: the design should speak, not the label. Like Anderson's approach to curation: the object should need no explanation.

我们不相信拥有你的 logo。我们相信服务于你的 form。FORMA 的标记是 subtle 的、建筑的、可选的。像 Ye 对 branding 的态度：设计应该说话，不是标签。像 Anderson 对策展的态度：物品应该不需要解释。

Do not try to please everyone. Some will say it is not formal enough for the office, not athletic enough for the court, not loud enough for the bar. They are right. It is none of those things. It is something else entirely. And for the man who recognizes what that something is — there is nothing else he would rather wear.

不要试图取悦所有人。有人会说它对办公室不够 formal，对球场不够 athletic，对酒吧不够 loud。他们是对的。它不是那些东西中的任何一个。它完全是 something else。而对于那个认出那个 something 是什么的男人——没有别的他更愿意穿。

— FORMA Atelier

We believe in access, not democracy. Democracy lowers the standard. Access raises the person. FORMA is not cheap. It is not precious. It is worthy of the life you are living.

我们相信access，不是 democracy。Democracy 降低标准。Access 提升人。FORMA 不便宜。它不珍贵。它配得上你正在过的生活。

The office does not own you. The court does not own you. The bar does not own you. Your clothes should not own you either. You are the CREATOR. The clothes are the VESSEL. The life is the FORM.

办公室不拥有你。球场不拥有你。酒吧不拥有你。你的衣服也不应该拥有你。你是 CREATOR。衣服是 VESSEL。生活是 FORM。
